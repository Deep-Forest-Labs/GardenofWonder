The questions this spike puts to the morning


1. The portraits sit beside every bubble, chat-style, rather than two fixed portraits at the
foot of the screen. Each row carries its speaker's face in that line's expression, so a swap reads as the face
changing mid-conversation. Reversal: fixed portraits at the foot, bubbles only in the stack.


2. Poppy in a villager's scene takes the other side for her line. Chapter I's scene is Mara and
Delphine, and Poppy speaks once; her line wears her own face on Delphine's side. Reversal: a third, smaller portrait.


3. The reply chips sit under the board, not under the bubble. Under the bubble is on top of
Poppy's face. Reversal: one number in the scene's CSS.


4. Tap-to-complete ships OFF. On, a fast tapper's second tap is spent finishing a slide; off,
every tap lands a line. The owner's standard is "never waits on an animation".


5. The title card is tap-to-pass, not timed: a timed card is a wait.
