The Record Shelf

Find a record, keep the song forever, and wear any charm with any tune. Nine sections. Every frame is a real 390×844 phone screen with a real iPhone's notch and safe areas. Nothing here is wired to the game — it is drawn from scratch so it cannot break when the game moves.

Sections 1–7 are the screens. Section 8 is the four rulings you said you might veto. Section 9 is five things the build found that the spec did not know — one of them stops Holly's Record working at all, and I need your word on it before I write the engine.

1 The shelf — three card states

The panel opens from either door. The equipped record sits at the top with both sides named even when one is empty, because two independent slots is the whole design and the header has to say so rather than leave you to infer it. Below, found records are wide rows; unfound ones are masked slots. A record is round, so the object is a disc — not a square card with a picture of a record on it.

2 The ??? slot: one hint, or five?

The spec says each unfound record carries "one directional hint". The shipped curtain does the opposite — every masked row in the game today shares one line, and the comment above it forbids per-row flavour on purpose. Both readings are drawn. This is a real choice and it is yours.

A One shared line (what the game does today)

For it: it is the law already on screen in the seed picker and the Almanac. A mask that hints is a mask with a leak — "reach Stand tier 3" tells you the Stand has a tier 3, which is the thing the mask is hiding.

Against it: five identical rows saying the same sentence reads as a wall, not as five secrets. The seed picker gets away with it because its rows differ in nothing but order.

B A line each

For it: records come from five completely different corners of the game — the Hollow, the Almanac, the Stand, Winter. A player who cannot tell them apart cannot be pulled toward any of them, and being pulled into every feature is what the shelf is for.

Against it: it is the first per-entry hint in the game, and it half-opens a curtain the rest of the game keeps shut. If you want it, the wording has to point at a direction without naming the thing — "someone in the Hollow has this one", not "get a creature to five stars".

3 The equip flow — Side A and Side B

Drawn in the Hollow's Loadout grammar: you arm a side, the header line says what a tap now means, and tapping a record swaps it into that side. The alternative — twin pills on every row, no mode at all — is drawn beside it.

4 The gramophone in the Hollow

The Decorate button stops apologising and does something. The gramophone is the room's first furniture and the first thing in this game that has ever spent a keepsake — priced as one keepsake each from two different creatures, so furnishing the Hollow rewards knowing more than one of them.

5 The drawer — Records, and Garden Journal

A fifth live row, a new vinyl glyph, and the reserved "Garden Record" row relabelled so two things in one menu are not both called records. The row and the rename are shown together, which is the only way to judge the rename.

6 The find, through the moments dialog

No new popup. A record find is a third kind of moment on the dialog the curtain pass already built — same card, same button, same queue, same one-at-a-time rule.

7 Every charm's line, beside Lucky Charm

All five charms in the words a player reads. Then the collision, in situ: the shop's Lucky Charm card and the quest strip's "Buy Lucky Charm", on screen next to a record's charm — so you can judge whether one word doing two jobs is a problem or not.

8 The four veto points

Ruled at spec time, flagged for you. Each is one line to reverse now and a rebuild to reverse later.

9 What the build found that the spec did not

Nine subsystems were read end to end before a line was written. Five things came back that change the spec. The first one is a blocker — it needs your word before the engine can be built. The rest I can decide myself if you would rather not spend the attention; my recommendation is on each.
