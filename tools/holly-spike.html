Holly — the winter rose. Character spike, slice C.

Four takes on Winter's hero flower, drawn to the Talking Flower's construction and judged beside her. The brief is the 2026-09-01 Holly ruling: pale porcelain face, near-black plum outer petals with hot-pink undersides, a frost crown of five points or more, angled lashed eyes, a smug smile, one hot-pink accent. Flat inks, the house outline, no translucency and no sparkle shaders — the ice lives in palette and shape. Her sass aims at the Summer flower, never at the player. Three rows below are gates rather than decoration: the 46px face row is the avatar picker's real size, the black-silhouette row is the ruling's own build gate, and the sleeping-plants row is new art with no stake attached.

3 · GATE — the black silhouette

Filled as a plain black shape, Holly must read as no one but herself. The ruling's own words: no two-point dark silhouette over a pale face — the crown carries five points or more, the forehead mark is winter botany, and there is no tail. The Summer flower is on the left so the pair can be told apart at a glance.

4 · New art — plants asleep under quilts

The sleeping grammar the creatures already have — shut-eye arcs, drifting Zs, the slow breathe — redrawn for a plant. Four studies of where the sleep actually lands. Nothing is staked on this: the tuck-in takes nothing away and adds nothing but the snowfall, so these must read as cosy, never as a warning.
