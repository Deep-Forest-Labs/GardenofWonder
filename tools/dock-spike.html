The Big Five — phase 3.5 wireframe spike

The dock and the bottom of the screen, rebuilt to the owner’s sketch: five main buttons — Orders & Quests · Cards · GARDEN · Turn · Shop — and one band above them holding UPGRADE, the tending creatures, and POWER-UP. Drawn at 390×844 with a real handset’s insets, using the metrics the running game actually reports, so the question does it fit is answered by measuring rather than by asserting. Static: no data.js, no game.js — every bloom, creature and icon here is a hand-drawn stand-in and every number is a placeholder. Wireframe fidelity: layout is the question, polish is the build’s job. Notes marked QUESTION are for the owner; notes marked DECIDED are calls I made and can unmake. Frame 1 is the one that matters most — every function on today’s dock and HUD, and where each one lands.
