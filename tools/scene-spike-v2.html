The three variants, frozen

A · Parity — the recording's shape, one to one

Every row a 96px face, ring and name pill in the speaker's colour, a white bubble bordered to match. Top-down fill. Mara's reply choices sit at the foot of the cutscene as two pills in her colour; the one tapped becomes her row. This is the closest to what you played.

B · Focus — the one speaking is the big face

Same as A, but only the newest row is full size (100px); older rows shrink to 64px and soften. Your note asked about the characters "that are speaking or thinking" — this makes the speaker unmistakable, at the cost of the recording's steady rhythm of equal rows.

C · Stage — two big busts at the foot, bubbles between

The two characters stand as large busts in the bottom corners for the whole scene — the speaker lit, the listener dimmed — and the bubbles carry a coloured name tab instead of a face. Fewer faces on screen, bigger art, and it needs Mara's full-length art sooner. Furthest from the recording.
