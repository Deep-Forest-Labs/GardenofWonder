The HUD's Top Row

The quest strip converts to the order strip at the first Turn and takes the level pill with it; the trunk's rail prints no digits. Reputation needs a home. The owner's fix: a compact level pill, top-left of the HUD, that an order's reputation flies into on delivery. This spike draws the recommended shape and the owner's own original proposal side by side, measures both for real at 390px and 360px with worst-case numbers in the wallets, and shows exactly what a third wallet costs — including a break this spike found live and did not expect to find.

Read the notes under each frame. Green = already ruled elsewhere; this spike follows it. Orange = an open question, the owner's call, the desk's recommendation marked as such. Blue = a placeholder — pixels had to be chosen to draw anything, not a recommendation. Red = something found live in the running game while building this, not proposed by anyone. Section 11 collects every decision, section 12 every question.

1 The problem, measured live

What is actually on screen today, and the real numbers everything below is built from — taken with getBoundingClientRect() against the running game, not estimated.

2 The recommended shape

Level pill + gold + gems + hamburger. The Almanac's dedicated HUD button retires — the drawer already has a row for it (section 8) — and Prisms stay on the Turn button's pouch chip where they already live. Two wallets, one compact pill, one button: lighter than today's two wallets and two buttons.

3 The owner's original proposal, drawn honestly

Level pill + gold + gems + a Prisms wallet + hamburger. Drawn at its real width, with the same worst-case numbers as section 2, so the third wallet's cost is visible rather than argued. Not softened, not picked against — measured.

4 Every arrangement, side by side

The same sums as sections 2 and 3, tabulated, plus today's HUD for reference — one table, both widths, worst-case numbers throughout.

5 The level pill, close up

Level number, progress ring, nothing else — reusing .q-pip-ring verbatim, per the brief. Beside it: a mechanism limit this spike found while reusing it, already live in the shipped game today.

6 The delivery animation — a storyboard

An order is filled → the reputation reward leaves the customer card → it flies to the level pill → the ring advances. The beat that turns a progress ring into a payout, so it gets the most care in this file. No timing is ruled anywhere below.

7 Before and after, same scale

Today's HUD against the recommended one, same 390px scale, ordinary numbers rather than the stress case — this is what the two actually look like day to day.

8 The hamburger menu, with the Almanac

Finding: the Almanac is already a row in this drawer. ui-menu.js's ROWS table has carried { id:'bonuses', icon:'book', label:'Almanac' } since 2026-08-31. "Moving it into the hamburger" costs no new menu work — only the HUD's own dedicated book button goes away.

9 The HUD across the conversion

The same recommended HUD in both states the quest strip ever wears: mid-tutorial, and after the first Turn. The level pill is the only thing on this row that survives the conversion unchanged underneath it.

10 The tree's door — one question, not answered

Open since spike v1: if the Almanac's petal tab is what the tree replaces, and the Almanac moves into the hamburger, where does the trunk's own door go? Three options, drawn side by side. None is picked here.
