The Curtain and the Drip

Twelve frames, 390×844, no game dependencies. Three surfaces: the seed picker's three bands, the upgrade shop's opening drip, and the moments dialog that celebrates both. Nothing here changes a single price, yield, grow time or unlock value — every number on every real (non-???) row is copied from the live game today. Green notes are decided calls, ready to build; orange notes are open questions; red notes are the two rulings this gate exists to let you veto.

1The seed picker — three bands, and the sort-sink rule

Unlocked rows first, exactly as today. Then the next locked seed — always revealed, always the advert. Then everything else: a silhouette, a name of ???, and one line that never says what it's hiding.

2The Almanac — masked, beside merely-unproven

Two kinds of "don't know yet," on purpose. A seed you've unlocked but never grown still shows its real name and art, dimmed — that's today's row and it doesn't change. A seed the curtain hasn't lifted on yet shows nothing at all.

3The upgrade shop — four cards, then a drip

The shop opens on exactly four badges. The rest queue up in the ladder's real order and step forward as lifetime gold clears their thresholds — never a purchase, never a Turn.

4The UPGRADE pill's dot — what it should mean now

Today the dot means one thing: something on the shelf is affordable. The curtain adds a second fact worth flagging — something on the shelf is new and you haven't opened the shop since. Four candidates, same pill.

5The moments dialog — one popup, three art tiers

Built once, reused for every reveal. It never waits on art: owner-supplied photo first, the shared placeholder second, the game's own bloom or badge last — and none of those three looks broken.

Decided in this spike (build these verbatim unless annotated otherwise)

  • The sort-sink rule. ??? rows never participate in Cheapest/Priciest/Balanced — they hold ladder (DATA.seeds) order and sit under every revealed row, always. Frame P2.
  • One hint line, not nineteen. Every ??? row reads the same line — "Keep growing — the garden isn't done with you." This mirrors the game's own existing masking grammar in pairRows() (an unformed creature pair shows "???" and one generic non-revealing note, never bespoke per-pair flavour text) rather than critterRows()'s per-creature hint field, which names a real creature that already has a face on screen. Cheap to build, nothing to author per seed, and it can grow bespoke lines later without a re-architecture.
  • The silhouette is generic, not per-species. One flat dark flower glyph for every masked row and every masked Almanac slot — never a shape that hints at family or rarity. The colour is the reveal; the shape stays a secret twice over.
  • Almanac masking keys to the same latch as the picker, never to discoveredOf. A never-grown-but-revealed seed keeps today's dimmed-but-real row exactly as is. Frame A1.
  • The moments dialog is drawn as literally #news, third mode — same card, same bullet grammar, same button, reusing its class names rather than inventing a sibling dialog system. Frames M1–M4.

Two rulings flagged for possible veto, per the spec

  • Arm 2 — the next locked seed is ALWAYS revealed, from a fresh save's first frame, regardless of the 85% earnings threshold. Without it there's a real recurring window right after any purchase where no advert shows at all — the wall's whole "six times everything I've earned" feeling would flicker on and off. Shown live in Frame P1/P3: Bluebell stays a priced advert even when Lavender is nowhere close to its own threshold. Veto this and the picker reads purely off the 85% line — simpler rule, but a save can go quiet stretches with nothing to save up for on screen.
  • Land Deed's reveal reads the Turn clock (turnsCompleted ≥ plotTurnGate) on top of the usual 400K lifetime gate — the one card in the whole drip that isn't earnings-only. Reasoning: it's structurally unbuyable before your first Turn (plots 5–8 stay gated), so revealing it on gold alone would pop a moment for a purchase the game refuses. Shown in Frame U3. Veto this and Land Deed reveals at 400K like every other card, showing a real price it can't yet spend — closer to "purely earnings-paced" as a rule, at the cost of one advert that lies a little about what today will let you do.

Open questions this spike is asking, not deciding

  • Which of the four UPGRADE-pill dot treatments in Frame D1 — plain reused, always numbered, two-tone, or no change at all? Doc 36 never specified the pill's dot colour or a numbered variant, so nothing here is a regression to preserve.
  • Button copy on the moments dialog. This spike reuses "Got it!" — the exact acknowledgement button the What's New dialog already trained players on — rather than inventing new copy for a dialog that, unlike that one, isn't asking the player to acknowledge anything. Worth a different word, or is reuse the point?
  • How many ??? rows a real fresh save should render before the list just keeps going — this spike shows five as a representative sample of the sixteen the real picker will draw. Not a design question so much as a "does this look right at full length" one; worth a look at the real nineteen-row build before the gauntlet.
