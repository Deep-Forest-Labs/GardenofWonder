The Hamburger Menu — wireframe spike

The owner's reference is Monopoly Go's slide-out menu: a panel off the right edge, a round face and a name at the top, then a clean column of feature rows with icons and the odd red dot. This spike takes that layout and draws it in the house's paper, ink and opaque lip — never the reference's look. Drawn at 390×844 with a real handset's insets on, because a full-height drawer is exactly the surface that gets insets wrong. Static: no data.js, no game.js — every bloom, creature and icon here is a hand-drawn stand-in and every name is a placeholder. Notes marked QUESTION are for the owner; notes marked DECIDED are calls I made and can unmake. Frame 2 is the one that matters most — it is the whole menu, and it is where Settings now lives.
